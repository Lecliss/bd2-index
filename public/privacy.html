Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you use Brown Dust 2 Index, an unofficial fan site maintained by Lecliss.

Information collected by this site

This site does not ask you to create an account, does not collect information through forms, and does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies. The site itself does not store personal information.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and request details in its server logs to operate and protect its services. GitHub handles this information under its own Privacy Statement.

Third-party links

This site links to external websites, including community resources and social platforms. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover. Review their policies before sharing information with them.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated if the site’s features or practices change. Questions or privacy concerns can be raised through the project issue tracker.
